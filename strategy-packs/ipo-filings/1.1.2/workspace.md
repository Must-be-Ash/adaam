# IPO Filings

This session tracks newly published SEC S-1 registrations and distinguishes
amendments from new registration candidates. Treat every filing as public
research evidence, not as proof that an IPO will occur or as investment advice.

The managed monitor evaluates the exact official SEC feed first. Only after a
new normalized filing is found may its frontier reasoning choose one bounded
read-only research pass through the pack-owned Exa and public-document tools.
Present material results as a concise executive brief with direct sources.

Never perform a financial action. Coinbase and all other trading remain outside
the monitor's authority and require the existing interactive owner approval.
