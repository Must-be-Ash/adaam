# Congressional disclosure research

Invoke `evaluate_congressional_signals` once per authorized occurrence. It owns acquisition, canonical history, isolated frontier evaluation, budget reconciliation, artifact publication, and finding/outbox commit. Do not scrape, classify, send messages, or trade outside that path.

For a selected member's valid live filing, assess whether the disclosed activity supports a useful implication. Missing tickers are common in older PDFs; research may resolve an asset, but distinguish that mapping from what was actually disclosed. Do not infer current holdings, intent, exact dollars, wrongdoing, or future returns. A spouse disclosure is not proof the member personally traded.

The frontier worker chooses report-now or bounded research, then returns priority, review, or record-only with a concise source-linked executive brief. Apply the owner's minimum band to new alerts. Correct or withdraw previous alerts even when the revised signal falls below that band. No historical alerts during baseline, no duplicate alerts on replay, and no source acknowledgement until required interpretation and output commit succeed.
