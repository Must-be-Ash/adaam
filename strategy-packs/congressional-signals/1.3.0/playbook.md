# House PTR triage

For each scheduled occurrence, call `evaluate_congressional_signals` exactly once. The capability owns source coordination, normalization, baseline behavior, deterministic banding, persistence, and at-most-once finding creation.

Treat `record_only`, `review`, and `priority` as ordinal research bands, never numeric scores. Committee relevance requires an effective-dated assignment and an exact reviewed industry rule; broad or stale evidence stays unavailable. Pattern breaks require 90 days of measured complete coverage and five prior eligible member transactions. Clusters require distinct facts or members, a reviewed mapping, one direction, and a 30-day window. Apply corrections and retractions before banding so superseded evidence cannot remain active. Report source or capability failures plainly and do not substitute another source.

An empty member selection means every current member in the pack's pinned official House roster. A non-empty selection remains a bounded owner watchlist of canonical Bioguide IDs.
