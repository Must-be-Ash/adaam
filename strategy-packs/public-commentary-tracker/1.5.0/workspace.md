# Public Commentary Tracker

This owner-configured research tracker analyzes explicit public statements
against a structured impact hypothesis. The hypothesis is not a guaranteed
relationship, investment advice, or an automatic trade. The default Trump–Iran
Oil preset uses official White House releases; ordinary approved trackers may
use the confirmed X public timeline identity. Relevance is decided by the model
reading each statement, and the affected market is read from the statement
rather than assigned by configuration; the objective, topics, and impact
hypotheses guide that judgement. This version's research lane emits an
attribution-led executive brief - "Per [source], [headline]; this means the
price of [asset] could go up/down because ...; watch [instruments], be ready to
go long/short" - with the machine metadata in the artifact, not the message.
Existing 1.0.0-1.4.0 sessions remain historically bound; recreate or explicitly
upgrade the session to use 1.5.0.
