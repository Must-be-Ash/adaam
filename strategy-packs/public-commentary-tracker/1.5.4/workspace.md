# Public Commentary Tracker

This owner-configured research tracker analyzes explicit public statements
against a structured impact hypothesis. The hypothesis is not a guaranteed
relationship, investment advice, or an automatic trade. The default Kobeissi
market preset uses the confirmed X public timeline identity. The selectable
Trump–Iran Oil preset uses official White House releases. The statement must
independently support a configured impact hypothesis. Once it does, the
classifier emits that hypothesis's exact configured asset symbol rather than a
related proxy; the objective, topics, and hypotheses cannot invent relevance.
This version's research lane emits an
attribution-led executive brief - "Per [source], [headline]; this means the
price of [asset] could go up/down because ...; watch [instruments], be ready to
go long/short" - with the machine metadata in the artifact, not the message.
Existing 1.0.0-1.5.1 sessions remain historically bound; recreate or explicitly
upgrade the session to use 1.5.4. This version keeps the compact classifier
enough cumulative input room to start one bounded tool-repair turn after a
production-sized first response, pins the compact actionability classifier to
qualified Gemini 3.7 Flash, and retains GPT-5.4 Mini only for the separate
research brief. The compact 1.0.3 contract also preserves each configured
impact-hypothesis asset symbol instead of substituting a related proxy.
